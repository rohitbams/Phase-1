These questions have legal and financial stakes. Legal advisor presence recommended.

Q1.1 — Klash Coin Wallet: Fiat vs Blockchain ✅ RESOLVED

Resolution Date: 27 May 2026
Source: RD document for Rohit Coin Wallet 27_5_2026.doc.pdf

✅ Confirmed Decision

1:1 fiat-backed wallet (NOT blockchain)
"The coin is a 1to1 fiat rate (currency) which is back by the users at phase 1"

Original Contradiction (Now Resolved):

  • BRD (3 Jan 2026, Page 5): "The coin should be based around Ethereum system. KO will create its own blockchain." → SUPERSEDED
  • Coin Wallet doc (27 May 2026): "The coin is a 1to1 fiat rate (currency) which is back by the users at phase 1" → CONFIRMED ✅

Implementation Confirmed:

  • ✅ Internal double-entry ledger (PostgreSQL)
  • ✅ Partner with licensed payment provider (Stripe/Mangopay) for FCA compliance
  • ✅ Self-custody wallet with fiat backing
  • ✅ Transaction types: Top-up, Gift, Purchase, Entry Fee, Winnings, Cash-out, Go Live, Nudge Play

See: Conflict Register C01, SRS Updates, Data Model

Q1.2 — Music Licensing Strategy

Source: Gaming Mechanic doc (music streaming in klashes) + Wallet & Coin UX doc (music purchase feature)

Background: KO streams music, clips snippets, and allows purchase. This creates licensing obligations.

Questions:

  1. What is the music library source at Phase 1 launch?
    • Licensed major-label catalogue (requires PPL/PRS licenses)
    • Independent Klasher uploads only (users own rights)
    • Hybrid
  2. If major-label: Has KO obtained licenses from PPL (Phonographic Performance Ltd) and PRS (Performing Right Society)?
  3. If user-uploaded: What rights verification process? (User attestation / Content ID matching / Manual review)
  4. Music purchase: How does KO handle mechanical licenses? (Partner with DistroKid/TuneCore / Direct agreements)

Recommendation: Start with Independent Klasher uploads only (Phase 1), add licensed catalogue later (Phase 2+). Drastically reduces legal complexity.

Q1.3 — Age Verification & Financial Features 🟡 HIGH

Source: Inferred from financial transaction features (coin purchase, cash-out) + UK regulatory requirements

Questions:

  1. What is the minimum age for KO? (13+ / 16+ / 18+)
  2. Should users under 18 be blocked from:
    • Purchasing Klash Coins?
    • Cashing out?
    • Super Klasher staking?
    • Go Live features?

Recommendation: 13+ for platform access, 18+ for all financial features. Aligns with UK gambling age restrictions.

Q1.4 — App Store In-App Purchase (IAP) Strategy 🟡 HIGH

Source: Meeting Minutes 5 March 2026 (future mobile app mentioned) + Industry knowledge of app store policies

Background: Apple/Google require digital goods purchased within apps to use their IAP system (15-30% commission).

Questions:

  1. Are you aware of this commission structure?
  2. Preferred approach:
    • Comply with IAP and absorb commission
    • Web-based purchase workaround (buy on website, use in app)
    • Price coins higher on mobile to offset commission
  3. When is mobile app expected to launch?

Recommendation: Web-based purchase initially. Compliant and avoids 30% commission.

Section 2: Game Mechanics & Voting 🟡 HIGH

Q2.1 — Winner Determination Logic

Source: BRD Page 4 ("Ending of a game") + Gaming Mechanic doc Step 9 (audience voting)

Background: BRD describes multiple ways a winner is determined (gifts, likes, comments, KO algorithm). Gaming Mechanic doc describes simple audience voting.

Questions:

  1. Primary result mechanism:
    • Simple audience vote (one vote per spectator)
    • Weighted scoring (gifts + likes + comments)
    • Hybrid (voting primary, algorithm fallback)
  2. If weighted scoring: What are the coefficients? (1 gift = X votes, 1 like = Y votes, etc.)
  3. What is the "KO algorithm"?
  4. Minimum audience size for valid vote?

Recommendation: Hybrid approach — primary is simple audience vote. If fewer than 5 spectators, algorithm decides (random selection initially).

Section 3: Scalability & Concurrency 🔴 BLOCKING

Q3.1 — Super Klash Participant Ceiling

Source: BRD Page 4 ("infinite number of players") + BRD Page 7 ("multiverse" concept for 100K+ users) + Gaming Mechanic doc Line 166 ("unlimited participants")

Background: BRD says klashes can have "an infinite number of players" and Super Klashers can host "unlimited participants." Technically, there must be a ceiling.

Questions:

  1. What is the realistic maximum number of participants for a single Super Klash at Phase 1 launch?
    • 100 (conservative, easy to deliver)
    • 1,000 (achievable, requires solid engineering)
    • 10,000 (ambitious, requires load balancing)
    • 100,000+ (requires "multiverse" sharding)
  2. The BRD describes an "infinite multiverse" concept where large events split into parallel simulations. Is this something you want?

Recommendation: Design for 10,000 participant ceiling at Phase 1 with horizontal scaling capability.
