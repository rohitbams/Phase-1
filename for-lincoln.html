What is KlashOut?

KlashOut is a music battle competition platform where users compete head-to-head in real-time "Klashes" and audiences decide winners through voting.

Core Concept

Two users select songs, play 30/60/90-second snippets, and spectators vote for the winner. Winners earn Klash Coins, climb leaderboards, and build their reputation.

Core Components

  • Music Library — Browse, search, upload, and stream tracks
  • Competitive Gaming — Head-to-head klashes, tournaments, and leaderboards
  • Social Network — Follow users, message, share playlists, comment
  • Digital Economy — Klash Coin wallet for prizes, gifts, and music purchases
  • Live Streaming — Artists perform live, earn from audience gifts

Three User Tiers

Tier 1: Klasher (Free)

Who: Casual fans and competitors

Can: Listen to music, compete in klashes, vote, earn coins from wins, spend coins in-app

Cannot: Upload music, cash out to bank, host group events, go live

Tier 2: Independent Klasher (Price TBD)

Who: Musicians and serious competitors

Can: Everything from Tier 1 plus upload music, monetize plays, host up to 50-person klashes, go live, cash out earnings

Cannot: Set entry fees, host tournaments, earn hosting fees

Tier 3: Super Klasher (Price TBD)

Who: Event organizers and influencers

Can: Everything from Tier 2 plus host unlimited-participant events, charge entry fees, run tournaments, earn automatic hosting percentage, access analytics dashboard

Cannot: Nothing — full access

Decisions Needed from You

These questions block Phase 2 planning. We need your input to proceed.

1. Tier Pricing

  • Monthly cost for Independent Klasher?
  • Monthly cost for Super Klasher?
  • Annual discount option?

2. Voting Mechanism

  • Is the winner decided by simple vote count or weighted algorithm?
  • If algorithm: what factors? (votes, gifts, comments, engagement?)
  • What happens in a tie?

3. Music Licensing

  • What music is in the library at launch? (User-uploaded only? Licensed catalog?)
  • Who handles licensing negotiations?

4. Wallet & Payments

  • Have you secured FCA legal advice on Klash Coin?
  • Which payment provider? (Stripe recommended)
  • Minimum cash-out amount?

5. Super Klasher Hosting Fees

  • What percentage does a Super Klasher earn from paid events? (10%? 15%?)

6. Music Earnings

  • Can Independent Klashers sell music directly to users?
  • Or do they only earn from streams and klash winnings?
  • Revenue split if selling? (70/30? 80/20?)

7. Age Restrictions

  • Minimum age to create account? (13+? 16+? 18+?)
  • Block users under 18 from wallet features?
