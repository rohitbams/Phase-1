3. Epic 4: Tournament Formats — Add Details

Source: RD document Rohit Klashing_Music (27 May 2026, Page 3)

The document provides specific tournament format descriptions. Update Epic 4 with:

Tournament Format Definitions

Round-Robin

Description: Every klasher plays every other at least once

Winner Determination: Player with most wins overall

Use Case: Small groups (8-16 players), ensures everyone gets equal play time

Swiss System

Description: Winners play winners, losers play losers

Pairing Logic: After each round, players with similar records are paired

Use Case: Medium-to-large tournaments (16-64 players), balances fairness with efficiency

Single/Double Elimination

Single Elimination: Immediate elimination after one loss

Double Elimination: Requires two losses to be eliminated (includes loser's bracket)

Use Case: Fast-paced, high-stakes tournaments

Free-For-All (FFA)

Description: Every player is an enemy, no teams

Special Rule: Can use up to 3 nudge plays in FFA mode

Use Case: Chaotic, high-energy events with multiple simultaneous competitors

4. Epic 10: Nudge Play — Add Mechanics

Source: RD document Rohit Klashing_Music (27 May 2026, Page 4)

Nudge Play Rules (PARTIAL ANSWER to Q2.2)

  • Mechanic: "Nudge klasher out" — interrupt an ongoing klash to replace a participant
  • Duration Restriction: Only available in 60 or 90 second klashes (NOT 30 second)
  • Timing Window: Can only be used halfway through the game (e.g., after 30 seconds in a 60-second klash)
  • Timing Constraint: Same as Go Live — can be used 10 seconds into klash but NOT in the last 10 seconds
  • FFA Mode: Can use up to 3 nudge plays in Free-For-All tournaments
  • Wallet Transaction: Costs Klash Coins (amount TBD with Lincoln)
  • Visual Feedback: When nudged, user's profile picture, username, and track info appear to opponent(s) and spectators

Updated User Story

F53 (Updated): Nudge Into Klash

As a Klasher, I want to interrupt an ongoing 60 or 90-second klash by nudging out a current participant so that I can take their place and compete for the win.

Acceptance Criteria:

Given a klash is in progress (60 or 90 seconds)
And the klash is at the halfway point
And I have sufficient Klash Coins
When I select "Nudge Play" and choose a participant to replace
Then the selected participant is removed from the klash
And I take their position
And my profile, username, and track are displayed to all participants/spectators
And the Nudge Play cost is debited from my wallet
